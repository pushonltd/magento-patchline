# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Email **hello@pushon.co.uk** with "Patchline security" in the subject.
Please don't open a public issue or pull request.

## Out of scope

Patchline downloads Adobe's patches and does not change them. A vulnerability in Magento itself, or in one of
Adobe's patches, goes to Adobe: <https://helpx.adobe.com/security/alertus.html>.
